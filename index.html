Start
Customer initiates the process by submitting a request to AYO.
AYO Verification
AYO verifies the customer's details and processes the request with the attached prescription.
Prescription Verification
Drugnet MCAs download claim information to verify the claim and prescription.
Google Sheet Entry
Customer details are filled in a Google Sheet, including names and other relevant information. A unique ID is generated for the customer for the transaction.
Generate Medicine List
MCA generates a list of medicines based on the verified prescription.
Pharmacist Approval
Drugnet Pharmacists approve the claim after reviewing the medicine list.
Update AYO Portal
MCA updates the AYO Portal with the final medication cost as per agreed pricing for Medicare.
Notify Customer
Customer is notified of the claim approval.
Delivery Location?
Check if the delivery location is Accra/Tema or elsewhere.
Package & Deliver
Medication is packaged, dispatched, and delivered to the customer in Accra/Tema.
Send Unique Code
Unique Claim Code with medication list is sent to customers outside Accra/Tema for pickup from a fulfillment partner.
Fulfillment Partner
Fulfillment Partner verifies the medication list and unique code via WhatsApp and serves the customer.
Daily Reconciliation
Daily Reconciliation report is sent to the Fulfillment Partner and settled as per agreed pricing.
End
Process completed successfully.
